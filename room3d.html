3D Room · beta

Design your room in 3D

Scan your room with your phone, then paint the walls, try a new floor, remove what you don't want and place new furniture. Everything changes live, like a game.

Scanning uses a phone's camera and motion sensors. On a computer, try the sample room or upload a 360 photo, or open this page on your phone.

  1. Scan. Dots on the screen show where to point. The page takes the photos by itself.
  2. Mark the corners. Tap the floor in each corner so we know the room's shape.
  3. Redesign. Tap a wall to paint it, change the floor, remove things, and shop real furniture in 3D.

Your photos stay on your phone. They're only sent out when you use Remove (to an AI image service) or make a product 3D model.
