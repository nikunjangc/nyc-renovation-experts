nyc-renovation-experts
A professional website for NYC Renovation Experts

## Partner Collection

`collection.html` and the `collection-*.html` pages show furniture from our
manufacturing partner. Every one of these pages ends with this disclaimer:

> **This is our partner collection.** The pieces on this page are made by our
> manufacturing partner and offered through NYC Renovation Experts. Photos are
> provided by our partner. Sizes, finishes, materials, pricing and lead times
> are confirmed with each order, so ask us for current details.

The partner's name and model codes never appear on the site; pieces use our
own numbers (Bed No. 01, ...). The pages and the Instagram posts on
`collection-posts.html` are generated by `tools/collection/`; see
[tools/collection/README.md](tools/collection/README.md).

## 3D Room

`room3d.html` turns a room into an editable 3D room: scan it with a phone's
camera (no app) or upload a 360 photo, mark the corners, then paint walls,
change the floor, remove objects (AI) and place furniture, including real
store products made into 3D. The page is kept out of search results while
it's tested. How it works and how to test it:
[tools/room3d/README.md](tools/room3d/README.md).
